Framebeats Local Studio

A personal music production tool

Framebeats Local Studio helps the owner of the FrameBeats YouTube channel research music topics, create original animated Shorts, schedule approved-by-workflow uploads, and evaluate channel performance. Access is intended for the channel owner.

What the tool does

The local workflow combines original English scripts, reusable Blender animation, ElevenLabs narration (currently limited to internal previews on the Free plan; public uploads require verified commercial rights), media checks, YouTube upload scheduling, and aggregate performance analysis. The owner authorizes the channel through Google OAuth.

Owner control

The operator can stop the daily task, revoke Google access, and review scheduled videos in YouTube Studio. Stopping the local task does not cancel videos already scheduled on YouTube.

Information and support

Read the Privacy Policy and Usage Terms below. For support, open an issue in the public information repository. Do not post passwords, keys, tokens or private channel data.
