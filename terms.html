Usage Terms

Personal workflow

Framebeats Local Studio is intended for use by the operator of the FrameBeats channel. These usage terms describe the tool and do not grant rights to third-party music, voices, footage or other materials.

Authorized content and accounts

The operator must use accounts they are authorized to manage, hold the required rights for published materials, and follow YouTube, Google API, OpenAI, ElevenLabs and hosting-provider rules that apply to their use.

Publication control

The operator may authorize automatic publication through the workflow. Media and editorial checks can stop a release. Technical checks do not establish copyright clearance, factual accuracy or artistic quality.

Service availability

Local operation depends on the Mac, application availability, account authorization, quotas and service availability. A render or scheduled upload does not establish that a video is public. The workflow checks publication status separately.

Monetization

Views, acceptance into monetization programs and revenue depend on platform decisions and audience response. Payment and tax arrangements must be handled by the eligible account holder; an approved parent or guardian AdSense account is required where the creator is under 18.

Stopping use

The operator can stop the task and revoke connected account access. Videos already scheduled on YouTube must be managed separately in YouTube Studio.

Contact and updates

For support, use the public information repository without posting private information. These pages should be reviewed when the workflow or its data practices change.
